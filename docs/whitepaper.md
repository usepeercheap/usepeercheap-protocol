# PEER Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Vision: a Solana where a large position can change hands as quietly as a small one.

Mission: give holders a clear place to see the market, find a counterparty, and settle wallet to wallet, so that conviction is expressed in size rather than in candles.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Public site, live market board, and the pinned slot for the PEER token. Brand and community channels opened.

**Phase 2: Launch.** Contract address published, token row goes live on the board, and links are connected.

**Phase 3: Matching.** A simple way for holders to post and answer offers, with every transfer approved by the wallets involved.

**Phase 4: Depth.** Wider token coverage, richer holder views, and tooling for larger positions.
