How it moves

Four beats from first look to final handoff.

Watch

The board ranks the busiest tokens and refreshes every minute.

Pick

Choose a token and copy its address with one press.

Meet

Reach the holder across from you through the channels listed in the token panel.

Settle

Each side approves the handoff from their own wallet.

Size without noise

Large positions change hands quietly, away from the order book.

Direct counterparties

You deal with a person, not a pool, and agree terms between you.

Open facts only

Every link and address on this page comes from one list of confirmed facts.

Plan

Four phases, one at a time.

Foundation

Public site, live board, and a reserved first slot for the token. Brand and community channels get set up.

Launch

The contract address goes public, slot one switches on, and the official links are connected.

Matching

A simple way for holders to post and answer requests, with every transfer needing sign-off from the wallets involved.

Depth

Wider coverage, richer holder views, and tooling built for larger positions.
